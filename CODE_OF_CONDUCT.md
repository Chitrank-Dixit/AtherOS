# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Examples of expected behavior include listening with empathy, giving constructive feedback, respecting different viewpoints, and focusing criticism on ideas rather than people. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the private channel used to provide this workspace. Maintainers will review reports confidentially and take proportionate action. This project is at its initial local foundation stage; no public reporting address has been established.
