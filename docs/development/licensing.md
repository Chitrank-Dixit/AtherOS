# Licensing and third-party notices

The repository's [MIT License](../../LICENSE) applies to original Aether software and documentation contributions, subject to each contributor's rights and applicable copyright notices. This clarification does not change the selected license.

Upstream operating systems, package contents, fonts, icons, firmware, codecs, and other third-party assets keep their respective licenses and attribution requirements. Inclusion in an Aether repository, profile, or image does not automatically relicense those works under MIT.

Before publishing any future ISO, produce and review an inventory of included packages and assets, include required license texts and notices in the image or accompanying materials, and provide attribution and corresponding-source information where the applicable licenses require it. The exact process remains to be designed before a public image release.
