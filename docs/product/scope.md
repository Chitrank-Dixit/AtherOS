# Product scope

## Long-term product
- Primary desktop selection among GNOME, KDE Plasma, and Hyprland; additional desktops may be installed later.
- Developer profiles for general development, Python, web, Go, AI/ML, and DevOps.
- Optional AI capabilities for development or productivity, with a no-AI installation path.
- Aether Studio for declarative configuration, desktops, profiles, applications, updates, and recovery.

## Sprint 0 scope
Establish repository practices, prototype experiment scaffolding, compare Arch and NixOS, and produce an evidence-based proposal. Do not implement an installer, Studio, the AI platform, production repositories, or a final base selection.
